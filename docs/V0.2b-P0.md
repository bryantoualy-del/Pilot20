# V0.2b.2 — executable P0 scope and limits

This stacked PR depends on V0.2b.1 (PR #3). It extends the **existing** transaction executor, not a parallel class-specific engine. No character/class/subclass identifiers drive execution. `examples/advanced-characters.mjs` supplies synthetic declarative profiles. Selected-target spell resolution is executable; no complete official spell catalogue is claimed.

## Contracts and compatibility

A permanent ability may opt in with `combat: {version:1,mode:'automatic'|'manual'|'referenced',requirements:[],...}`. The versioned schema is `schemas/combat-rules-v1.schema.json`. V2 character/session envelopes and V1 low-level session envelopes remain compatible through optional fields. Existing V1 sessions and immutable receipts retain their original compiled mechanical definitions. No inference upgrades an old descriptive spell to an executable complete spell. Newly compiled V2 `kind:'spell'` entries require explicit spell profiles; entries without them remain represented. `manualPlan` preserves descriptive effects/conditions/triggers in compiled manual definitions and never executes them.

`spell` declares stable spell ID, casting source, base level and access (`known`, `prepared`, or feature `granted`). The compiler checks the relevant character list and the level available to that casting progression, rather than shared multiclass slot level. Granted features may explicitly grant higher-level access. Preparation counts, official class lists, spellbook-copy legality, components, range, line of sight, creature eligibility and environmental consequences are not inferred. V0.2c must validate them from pinned catalogue grants. Fixtures are synthetic selected-effect scenarios, not automatically certified official spell lists.

Ordinary and pact resources carry independent `slotLevel` and `pool`. `slotId` selects an existing pool with sufficient level; no second payment through the old fixed `resource` field is allowed on a spell. A higher slot is consumed at that level. Extra dice are added only with `upcast:{effectIndex,dicePerLevel}` and validated final dice. Unknown scaling is not invented. Rituals, long casting, slot conversion, arcanum exceptions and recovery allocation remain manual/deferred.

## Commands

All commands retain `id`, type, actor references and optional `expectedRevision`. Identical receipts replay without mutation; collisions reject. Revision checks apply to fresh commands; a replay returns its original result even after later revisions. Invalid commands mutate neither input nor persisted state. Encounter authority is required before replay or mutation. GM-authored outcome/turn/correction commands additionally require GM role in Encounter mode.

| Command | Important inputs | Result / behavior |
|---|---|---|
| `useAbility` | `abilityId`, target IDs, injected attack/save/effect rolls; optional `slotId`, `budget` | Activation → action/resource payment → rolls → immediate effects → sustained effects; transaction rolls back on malformed input |
| `startTurn` | actor | Implicitly closes previous open turn once; ticks end/start boundaries; resets own action/reaction/movement/grants; clears all actors' current-turn spell ledgers |
| `endTurn` | active actor | Ticks end once; closes own turn; expires turn-end action inhibition |
| `move` | distance | Tracks distance spent against current modified speed and Dash count; never moves an actor on a map |
| `endConcentration` | source actor | Ends concentration and removes dependent effects across all targets |
| `removeEffect` | runtime effect ID, reason | Traceable manual removal, including end consequences |
| `declareManual` | manual ability, targets, slot, reason | Pays once, starts activation concentration where declared, persists an unresolved declaration |
| `resolveManual` | declaration ID, reason, ordered outcomes | Applies only explicit supported outcomes, then closes declaration atomically |
| `undo` | latest transaction ID | Restores every affected runtime field; immutable replay receipts remain tombstones |

Legacy `damage`, `correct`, `rest`, `setIncapacitated`, `concentrationSave` remain. Resolve timed effects before resting: the executor rejects a rest while timed effects are active instead of silently keeping buffs through an hour. Out-of-combat elapsed-time advancement is deferred. A pending manual declaration blocks new dependent commands; only its resolution or undo is allowed. Replays remain harmless.

## Saves and spell timing

`save:{ability,dc,onSuccess:'half'|'none'}` uses one injected `saveRolls[targetId]` per target and derived save modifiers. Natural 1/20 are numeric saving throws, not automatic outcomes. Advantage and disadvantage cancel. Damage dice for the same area activation are shared, with target-specific saves. Typed damage is aggregated before reduction; save reduction precedes resistance/immunity/vulnerability, with floors at each prescribed step. Persistent rules and immediate effects can declare `when:'success'|'failure'|'always'`; persistent effects default to failure on a save. Effects without a save apply normally.

A valid cast consumes its slot even if every target saves or an attack misses. A malformed command rejects atomically, including all spends. Concentration starts once per activation before targets, retains self-damage checks, and replaces only its old instance. A source-owned concentration duration clock exists independently of targets, including when no target receives a lasting effect. Failed concentration, zero HP or incapacitation removes all linked effects. A short individual effect may expire without prematurely ending the longer spell.

Casting history is per **current turn**, including off-turn reactions. Casting a bonus-action spell permits only action cantrips as other spells in that turn, in either order. Two levelled action spells with an unrestricted granted action are permitted by the 2014 rule. Metamagic is not enabled by changing a command's timing; Quickened/Heightened require the deferred modifier/decision executor and remain blocked.

## Effects, stacking and time

Runtime `advanced` owns effects, grants, cast ledger, movement, duration clock and manual declaration. Permanent `definition` is never edited by buffs. Each effect has an instance ID, source actor, optional concentration instance, timing anchor, remaining boundary count and original rule. Modifiers cover AC, speed, attack, damage and six saves; save advantage/disadvantage and explicit action/casting/concentration/movement inhibition are supported. Other D&D conditions (paralysis, prone, frightened, etc.) are **not** implied by a text label and must remain represented/manual until all consequences exist.

Stack policy is explicit: `independent` applies all declared independent sources; `priority` keeps all instances but selects highest declared priority (latest on ties); `replace` removes old instances. Same-key conflicting policies and incompatible simultaneous `set` modifiers reject. Priority is authored by rules data/MJ; the engine does not guess spell potency or D&D stacking legality. Distinct compatible modifiers apply set, then addition, then multiplication, then floor. `onEnd` consequences are explicit data. Same-key suppressed copies do not grant duplicate actions or end a surviving effect when one source expires.

Durations use logical source/target boundaries. `turn` and `round` count the specified anchor's start/end; a minute is ten such round cycles. The combat controller must emit logical turn boundaries in initiative order, including for skipped/incapacitated actors, and must not advance a source twice within one ordinary round. `endTurn` is optional because `startTurn` closes the previous open turn, and explicit end calls are never counted twice. This engine is not a real-time clock, initiative scheduler, time-stop handler or out-of-combat timer. Alternative schedules require manual resolution; do not infer elapsed minutes from arbitrary repeated user turn changes.

## Action and movement budgets

Normal Attack actions retain V0.1 attack counts. Extra full actions have independent remaining attacks and may interleave with normal attacks. A data-defined resource-backed grant adds one full action, with a per-turn use limit; it does not refresh bonus action, reaction, or previous attacks. Restricted effect budgets accept only listed action kinds and one declared weapon attack. The sample Haste rule grants exactly weapon attack / Dash / Disengage / Hide / Use an Object, plus +2 AC, doubled speed and advantage on DEX saves. Magic-item activation/spellcasting are not Use an Object. Tactical outcomes of Hide/Disengage/object use remain adjudicated; this primitive validates their budget, not terrain or perception.

Ending the sample effect removes all benefits and prevents actions, bonus actions, reactions and movement until the end of the target's next turn. Remaining granted actions disappear on expiry. Movement uses current speed × (1 + Dash count), so a speed change also changes Dash movement; terrain costs, opportunity attacks, reach, teleport destinations and map coordinates are external/manual.

## Safe manual procedure and decisions

Choose an explicitly manual profile; a failed automatic profile cannot fall back silently. Declare the ability with the targets, slot and a nonempty legality/adjudication reason. The original plan is retained, and declaration spends resources once. Resolve with nonempty global and per-outcome reasons. Supported outcomes are **already adjudicated final** damage, capped healing, max temporary HP, bounded resource correction, supported effect addition/removal and explicit incapacitation. Final damage is not reduced a second time. Unknown outcomes or undeclared targets reject the entire resolution, even after earlier staged outcomes. Narrative outcomes may be recorded with no mechanical changes.

Undo resolution restores the paid pending declaration. Undo that declaration next restores its resources/economy/concentration; later events cannot be silently rewound. This is intentionally two committed steps, with separate immutable IDs. A response after reload cannot pay or apply twice. Manual hit-gated concentration remains unsupported; a manual transformation is a narrative/adjudicated procedure, not an automated substitute form or HP swap.

The pending manual declaration is the only executable decision workflow in this PR. The generic reaction/post-hit continuation contract in RULE-CONTRACTS.md remains structural. Shield/Counterspell/Sneak Attack/Divine Smite, full Rage, metamagic, bardic support dice and transformations are not enabled. Ki and Channel resources use the shared pool/rest contracts, but their class-specific action combinations are not advertised as complete.

## Integration and performance

RPG Connect will transport commands/events; Encounter remains authoritative. No network implementation is included. Direct low-level `execute` assumes its pinned mechanical definitions were validated by an appropriate adapter; the V2 compiler is the known/prepared boundary. Callers in connected mode must route final Encounter outcomes once, not run a local automatic damage command and replay its result as damage again.

V0.3 can render declared actions, budgets, sources, effect expirations, coverage badges and the manual declaration/resolution flow. It must disable represented automatic abilities and avoid describing partial whole spells as automated. Generic decision and transformation forms remain disabled until V0.2b.3. History still clones full snapshots and retains receipts without eviction; long-session performance work remains V0.2c. No Safari/iPad, live transport, OS-crash or real storage-quota claim is made by Node/fake-indexeddb tests.
