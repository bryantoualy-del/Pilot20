# Known limits after follow-up

The implemented V0.2c scope is ready for review with documented manual adjudication. This is not a claim that the entire D&D ruleset or either expansion is automated.

- The public census remains 12 classes with twenty acquisition rows each, 12 SRD subclasses, 319 spell descriptions and 298 feature/option entries. An exhaustive denominator for every nested official feature is not claimed.
- Precise monk tools, ranger languages and favored humanoid races are now explicit selections. Whether a language is actually spoken by the chosen enemy remains a stated GM eligibility check. Origin/background construction is outside V0.2c; declared background proficiencies can be supplied and the ancestry placeholder remains visible.
- Optional level snapshots now validate learning, wizard book retention/acquisition, known-spell/invocation replacement budgets and multiclass chronology. Without snapshots, historical legality is explicitly `not-recorded`. Transcription costs/time and approved retraining remain adjudicated. Complete build-plan exports preserve previous choices; current-character choice notes preserve compact acquisition deltas.
- Mastery, Signature Spells, Arcanums and ritual-only acquisitions have explicit slot-free policies at their real levels. Signature/Arcanum charges are tracked. Components, geometry, utility/narrative results and manual Counterspell interaction still require GM resolution. Long casting time is a confirmed manual completion, not a simulated clock.
- Manual feature profiles now track action/resource costs for common class/subclass features. Tracking the cost does not automate Wild Shape, summons, auras, contested checks or outcomes. Alternate costs such as the astral Empty Body option remain stated manual adjustments.
- Calendar-sensitive recovery (Arcane Recovery's daily use, Divine Intervention's result-dependent cooldown) never resets through ordinary rest. The GM confirms elapsed time and records a correction. This avoids implementing an unrequested calendar simulation.
- Generic optional-feature acquisition, replacement, obsolete choice/resource suppression and conflict checks are supported. Replacing a capability with new mathematical/effect semantics still needs a reviewed adapter or an explicit manual adjustment. Existing combat instances remain revision-pinned.
- The Artificer is not public official content here. Optional round-up multiclass contribution/start-level policies are supported; infusion/item binding and specialization behavior still need authorized private definitions and manual/reviewed adapters.
- No iPad/browser measurement or visual UX claim. The benchmark files identify the Node host and exclude JSON module parse from warm index timing.

No animations, UI, V0.3 implementation, automatic merge or deployment are included. The appropriate next step is review of the current PR and acceptance of explicit manual workflows before freezing the V0.3 contracts.
