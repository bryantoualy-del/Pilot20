## Review before freezing the V0.3 contract

The gate remains **partial-review-required**. `v0.2c-limitations.md` lists the remaining manually reviewed nested choices, learning/retraining history, subclass counters, complex recharge and slot-free casting policies. The test suite being green does not establish exhaustive character legality. The class/spell corpus is ready to review; the full V0.2c acceptance gate must not be announced closed without reviewing these limits.

Concrete V0.3 preparation: expose `issues` and `manualRequirements` together, require explicit ancestry/background, use one shared resolver for Guided/Rapid/Expert modes, keep authored templates distinct from automatic choices, and build the single vertical path “create → save → open Companion → local combat”. Do not expand backend automation indefinitely; decide and expose acceptable manual workflows first. No V0.3 implementation is included here.
