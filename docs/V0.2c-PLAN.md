# V0.2c — catalogue integration plan

Precondition: review/merge V0.2b.1 then V0.2b.2. Keep V0.2b.3 reaction/post-hit automation separately reviewable; until it passes its own tests, dependent content is manual/unsupported. No UI work starts in these lots.

1. **Pin legal source and catalogue manifest.** Import SRD 5.1 CC BY 4.0 only, with exact attribution, original stable IDs, source page references, version and changes. Maintain homebrew/private/third-party packs independently. License review precedes public inclusion; neither a URL nor a `private` metadata label grants redistribution permission. Unknown third-party licenses remain blocked pending a reviewed policy.
2. **Define typed content mechanics per item type.** Extend inert rules-catalog imports with strict per-type mechanical schemas. Validate cross-references and dependency cycles atomically before installation; reject future rulesets, callbacks, unsafe keys and unknown effects without dropping data. Provide dry-run diagnostics and whole-pack rollback. Do not treat successful inert pack import as permission to execute its mechanical payload.
3. **Add twelve level 1–20 class grant tables.** Include class/subclass choices, ASIs, spell learning/preparation counts, independent resources, prerequisite AND/OR checks, starting versus multiclass proficiencies, non-stacking Extra Attack and separate ordinary/pact pools. Table-driven tests must inspect every level and relevant multiclass thresholds. Resolve known/prepared/book/always-prepared access per class, including Secrets, invocations and oath/domain grants; never grant a spell from a mere catalogue reference.
4. **Import representative spell families before bulk expansion.** For each spell store all required consequences: eligibility, components, cast time, range/area, duration, saves, dice/scaling, concentration, repeated triggers, environmental effects and necessary choices. Map only supported consequences to P0 profiles. Full spell status remains partial/manual when geometry, components or environmental/narrative consequences are essential and unresolved. Fixture selected-target success is not full spell certification.
5. **Enable manual content deliberately.** Generate explicit manual declaration plans for narrative/complex spells with costs, legal access, reasons and supported bounded outcome edits. Preserve unsupported original mechanical intent. Never replace an unsupported transformation/summon with a fake hit-point or damage operation. Expose structured missing-primitive diagnostics and exact player/MJ responsibilities.
6. **Coverage and validation gates.** Pin denominator to catalogue pack IDs/versions. Report references, manual/partial/automated/unsupported whole abilities and primitive resolution separately; include twelve-class structural tests, actual combat families and missing dependencies. `coverage:check` must fail for stale generated reports. Enable a spell only after legal source, compiler legality and consequence tests pass; add regression seeds for each family and edge case.
7. **Long-session/storage work.** Benchmark full snapshot cloning and receipts on long fights, add paginated/delta history and checkpoint recovery without receipt eviction, test conflict/crash/quota behavior, and measure iPad Safari/IndexedDB. Keep claims tied to actual environments. Existing CAS and undo/replay semantics must survive any storage migration.

Acceptance: all twelve class progressions have validated tables and tests; all imported items have provenance and explicit whole-mechanic status; incomplete effects remain non-automatic; no lost imports, resources, receipts or pinned definitions; no cloud, accounts, public deployment or V0.3 interface is included.
